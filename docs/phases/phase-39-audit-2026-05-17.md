**OutOfScope reasons reference promotion slices that shipped without promoting them.**

- P38: 38K promised to promote 3 `jobs.*` rows; shipped without promoting. Tightened in P38-D to name the specific filings.
- P39: 39L promised to promote 6 `auth.*` / `tenant.*` / `approval.*` rows; shipped (the CLI wiring) without promoting any of the rows. Will tighten in 35V2-P39-B.

The 35-N audit-correction slice (2026-04-29) added these OutOfScope rows as placeholders naming "the audit-correction slice that promotes." The audit-correction slices then shipped their stated runtime surfaces, but **did not own the registry-row promotion as part of "phase done."** The verification-round audits are catching this systematically.

**Pattern worth pinning for P40-P42 audits + learnings.md:** when a phase has OutOfScope rows that name a future slice for promotion, the future slice must explicitly tick the row (promote OR tighten reason). The current "future slice promotes" reason on a row whose future slice already shipped is itself drift.

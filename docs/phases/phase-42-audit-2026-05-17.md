## Systemic finding

Phase 42 surfaced the **largest filing tail of any P38-P42 audit** because the per-app maturity work is fundamentally launch-readiness work — turning reference shapes into shipped-ready products with full runbooks, full eval coverage, full adversarial corpora, claim files, benchmark files, AI helpers. P42's slice checklist closed the SHAPE; the v1.0 launch needs the SUBSTANCE.

This isn't drift the verification round failed to catch — it's drift the verification round is *uniquely positioned* to surface, because each per-app maturity gap is binary and easy to measure. The audit's job is to make the gap visible; the launch-readiness window's job is to close each one.

Pattern to pin in learnings.md when round closes: **reference-app phases need a separate "per-app maturity gate" slice before phase-done can tick.** Phase 42 shipped the shape but not the substance; the verification round caught it; future reference-app phases should ship a maturity-validation slice as part of the phase, not after.
